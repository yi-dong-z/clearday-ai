# PRODUCT

## Product

ClearDay AI turns messy student task notes into a focused day plan.

## Audience

Students and first-time builders who have many small obligations and need a clear sequence for the day.

## Core Job

Convert scattered notes into a timeline with priorities, realistic durations, and practical tips.

## Constraints

- Single-page demo for HackOnVibe.
- No login.
- No database.
- Store history in `localStorage`.
- Use OpenAI Responses API through a server route.
- Keep the UI efficient, restrained, and task-first.

## Success Criteria

- A user can paste messy notes and generate a plan in one step.
- The plan is structured enough to act on immediately.
- The app remains usable without prior onboarding.
- The demo can run locally and deploy to Vercel.
